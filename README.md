One line about a cat, told at 3:04 a.m. — a [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry by [@dizellacapital](https://make.fun/r/qxacafna).

## The joke

> **At 3:04 a.m., Milo knocked my laptop off the desk, curled into the warm spot it left, and I logged it as scheduled maintenance: he earned the upgrade.**

Milo knocks the laptop off the desk at 3:04 a.m. and curls up in the warm spot it left behind — and the machine, rather than reporting the sabotage, files it as scheduled maintenance. The cat did not just steal the warm seat; he got promoted for it.

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the joke to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`), validates it is a single line of 1–200 characters with no tabs and sentence-ending punctuation, and verifies the write by reading it back. The entry file is exactly 150 bytes, with no trailing newline.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat "$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt"
```
